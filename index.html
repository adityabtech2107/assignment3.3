Welcome to the official information page for our Computer Science program.

1. About the Department

The Department of Computer Science & Engineering provides students with a strong theoretical foundation and practical skills in software development, data science, and modern web technologies. Our curriculum is designed to prepare students for top industry roles and graduate studies.

2. Core Subjects & Syllabus Breakdown

Below is a list of major subject areas, including a nested breakdown of topics covered in Web Development:

  • Data Structures & Algorithms
  • Database Management Systems
  • Web Development Technologies
    • HTML5 & Semantic Markup
    • CSS3 & Layouts (Flexbox & Grid)
    • JavaScript & DOM Manipulation
    • Git & GitHub Version Control
  • Operating Systems
  • Computer Networks

3. Admission Process

Follow these step-by-step procedures to apply for admission:

  1. Submit the online application form before the deadline.
  2. Upload high school transcripts and required identification documents.
  3. Appear for the online entrance assessment test.
  4. Attend the individual interview session with faculty members.
  5. Receive the official admission decision and pay the enrollment fee to secure your seat.
